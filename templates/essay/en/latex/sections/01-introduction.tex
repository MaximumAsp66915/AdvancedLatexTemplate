\section{Introduction}

Open with the question or puzzle the essay addresses and say why it matters. Then state your thesis
clearly and outline the structure of the argument. Paragraphs are indented and justified; no blank line
is needed between them.

A short quotation can frame the discussion:
\begin{quote}
  ``The purpose of computing is insight, not numbers.''\\
  \hfill\emph{R.~W.~Hamming}
\end{quote}

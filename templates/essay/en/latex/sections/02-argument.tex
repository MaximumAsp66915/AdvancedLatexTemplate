\section{The Argument}

\subsection{First Claim}
Develop each claim in its own subsection: make the point, give evidence and explain how the evidence
supports the claim. Cite sources \cite{strunk1999} and add side remarks as footnotes.\footnote{A footnote
gives context without interrupting the argument.}

\subsection{Second Claim}
Build on the first claim. Equations, when needed, are numbered:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:example}
  \text{persuasion} = \text{evidence} + \text{reasoning}.
\end{equation}
Refer to them as \cref{eq:example}.

\subsection{Objections and Replies}
Consider the strongest objection to your thesis and answer it honestly.

\begin{notebox}[Style]
Prefer short sentences, concrete nouns and active verbs. Read the essay aloud before submitting it.
\end{notebox}

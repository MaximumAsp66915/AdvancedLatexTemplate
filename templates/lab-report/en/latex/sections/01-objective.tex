\section{Objective}

State what the experiment is meant to show and what you will measure, in two or three sentences.
For example: to verify the voltage-divider rule experimentally, to measure the loading effect of a
voltmeter, and to estimate the uncertainty of the result.

\begin{notebox}[Goals]
\begin{itemize}
  \item Verify $V_\mathrm{out}=V_\mathrm{in}\,R_2/(R_1+R_2)$.
  \item Measure how a finite load resistance changes the divider output.
  \item Report every result with its uncertainty.
\end{itemize}
\end{notebox}
